About John

An operator who's been
in the room when it counts

Most advisors will tell you they know how to grow a business. John Weber has actually done it, multiple times, across multiple industries, in the gritty Small to Medium Business space where the playbook isn't always clear and the margin for error is real.

From his early career in sales and engineering to leading organizations as President, co-founding ventures from scratch, and building an ownership stake in commercial services businesses, John brings something rare to the table: genuine operator instincts developed through decades of running businesses and advising business leaders in the $3-$450M revenue range.

He doesn't just understand strategy in theory. He's lived through the hiring challenges, the cash flow crunches, the sales cycles that stall, and the pivots that save a company. That experience is exactly what makes him a valuable partner for investors, boards, and business owners who are building something serious.

Today, John is selectively pursuing board seats, investment partnerships, and coaching engagements where his experience can directly accelerate growth. If you're working on something in industrial services, commercial trades, or the broader Small to Medium Business space, he'd like to hear about it.
